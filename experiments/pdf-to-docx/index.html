◇ PDF processing stays on your device. How it works

Convert PDF to editable Word locally

Rebuild readable PDF text, supported tables, photos and document artwork into a downloadable DOCX without uploading the PDF to a server.

What stays editable?

Readable text and detected table structures are rebuilt as Word content. Photos, logos, barcodes and unsupported visual elements can remain artwork so content is not silently lost.

What can still shift?

Font substitution, fixed-position designs and complex PDF layouts can reflow when opened or edited in Word or WPS.
